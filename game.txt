INPUT - 2 modes depending on plane's height

1. Below top of skyscrapers (similar to rebel assault speeder)
                 - UP/DOWN changes Height
		 - LEFT/RIGHT = yaw
  
2. Above skyscraper tops (looking down) movement is similar to games like 1942
   ie a top down shoot-em-up.

- When you are flying above the skyscrapers you have very little danger to
yourself and crashing into buildings.
- It is SIGNIFICANTLY easier to kill monkeys with top down shoot-em-up mode
- You can't rescue blondes in this mode.

However, the longer you fly above the skyscrapers, a bar fills up, when it does
The Giant Claw, a huge bird shadow appears over the city, you can dodge it but
if the shadow covers you, you die.
Thus pressure is put on you after a while to either evade (which is difficult)
or fly down into the skyscrapers again, making shooting harder and putting
yourself in danger of crashing into buildings.

Some monkeys are innocent but your primitive technology to tell good ones and
bad ones apart isn't 100% accurate.
Sometimes in your haste you kill good ones by mistake. If another
monkey sees you kill an innocent one, he will get angry and grow, and
potentially kill.

Little (mostly innocent) monkeys wander around the street.

Medium monkeys attack you, grab blondes from windows and climb to the top of
skyscrapers

Large monkeys go on rampages and can stomp buildings.

The game is about escalation. From the start you can be careful and kill only
the bad monkeys, if you are sloppy the good monkeys will turn, the monkeys
will grow and the violence will escalate. Eventually if you screw up you can
have monkeys the size of buildings totally trashing the city.

Tally of:
blondes saved vs died
innocent monkeys killed
monkeys killed
skyscrapers destroyed.
